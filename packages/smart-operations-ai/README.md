# @kva/smart-operations-ai

Explainable, deterministic operational intelligence for KVA OS.

## v1.0 rules

- Event Platform degradation
- Stalled active flights
- Missing aircraft assignments
- Completed flights without PIREPs
- Delayed PIREP review
- Aircraft operational-state gaps

Smart Operations AI is airline-management intelligence. It does not replace
the future conversational AI Dispatcher or the pilot-facing Digital Flight
Companion.
