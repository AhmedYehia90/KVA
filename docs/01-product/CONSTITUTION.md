# Constitution

1. KVA is a virtual aviation platform.
2. The pilot is the hero.
3. Every flight changes something meaningful.
4. AI assists, teaches, and recommends.
5. Progress is permanent.
6. History is archived, not erased.
7. Legacy is earned.
8. Every domain has one source of truth.
9. Capabilities communicate through versioned events.
10. Major capabilities are documented before implementation.
11. Releases must be testable and reversible.
12. Secrets and sensitive data are never committed.
