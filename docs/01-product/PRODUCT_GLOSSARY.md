# Product Glossary

- **Moment**: a permanent meaningful milestone, not a generic badge.
- **Legacy**: the long-term impact of a pilot, aircraft, airline, airport, or event.
- **Capability**: a complete product function spanning rules, data, events, APIs, UI, and tests.
- **Pillar**: a strategic user-facing product promise.
- **Engine**: a domain service responsible for authoritative decisions and state transitions.
