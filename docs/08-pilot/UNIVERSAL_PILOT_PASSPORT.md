# Universal Pilot Passport

The Universal Pilot Passport (UPP) is the pilot's platform identity across
every airline operating on KVA OS.

## Identity

- One stable passport number per pilot
- One shareable public slug
- Private, network and public visibility
- No email or private account data is exposed publicly

## Portability

- Airline memberships remain attached to the platform identity
- Qualifications can be recognized by one or more organizations
- The experience ledger follows the pilot between airlines
- Original PIREPs remain the source of truth

## Routes

- Owner view: `/pilot/passport`
- Public view: `/passport/[slug]`
