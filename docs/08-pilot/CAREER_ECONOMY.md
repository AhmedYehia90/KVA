# Career & Economy

Pillar 08 separates pilot progression from company economic authority while
reconciling both through one append-only Economy Ledger.

## Pilot side
- Career XP, flight totals and rank progression
- Evidence-backed salary and performance bonus
- Pilot Wallet
- Pilot Marketplace for personal/cosmetic progression items only
- Route-support contributions as demand/interest signals
- Career milestones and promotion history

## Company side
- Company treasury
- Operations Economy Console
- Company Marketplace
- Aircraft purchase / lease economic decisions
- Company assets
- Route-support review
- Salary/reward policy administration
- Admin audit history

## Authority boundary
Pilots cannot purchase aircraft, lease aircraft, modify the fleet or open
routes. Route support can reach 100%, but Operations must still make and record
the final company decision.

Company Marketplace aircraft purchases create an economic asset and ledger
record. They do not automatically create or modify a fleet registration.

## Integrity
- No flight → no salary.
- No evidence → no reward.
- No ledger entry → no balance change.
- Pilot support → interest signal, not operational authority.
- Fleet decisions → company authority only.
