# KVA Product Bible

## Mission
Give every virtual pilot a platform on which to build a professional aviation career and leave a lasting legacy inside a living aviation world.

## Vision
Become the global reference for virtual aviation career management, airline operations, intelligent guidance, community history, and connected flight experiences.

## Product promise
Build Your Aviation Career.

## Philosophy
Every flight matters. Every pilot has a story. Progress is permanent. AI teaches rather than replaces. The world continues to evolve even when a user is offline.

## Core loop
Login → Book Flight → Dispatch → Operate Flight → Debrief → Career Progress → Moment → Legacy → Airline Story → Next Flight.

## North Star Metric
Legacy Moments Created.

## Design law
No orphan features.
