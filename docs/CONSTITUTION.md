# KVA Constitution

1. KVA is a virtual aviation platform, not only a virtual airline website.
2. The pilot is the hero of the product.
3. Every completed flight must change something meaningful.
4. AI assists, teaches, and recommends; it does not remove human judgment.
5. Progress is permanent and portable where policy permits.
6. History is archived rather than erased.
7. Legacy is earned.
8. Every domain has one source of truth.
9. Capabilities communicate through versioned domain events.
10. Every major feature must be documented before implementation.
11. Every release must be testable and reversible.
12. Secrets and personal data are never committed to source control.
