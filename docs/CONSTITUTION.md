# KVA Constitution

1. KVA is a virtual aviation platform, not only a virtual airline website.
2. The pilot is the hero of the product.
3. Every completed flight must change something meaningful.
4. AI assists, teaches, and recommends.
5. Progress is permanent.
6. History is archived rather than erased.
7. Legacy is earned.
8. Every domain has one source of truth.
9. Capabilities communicate through versioned domain events.
10. Major capabilities are documented before implementation.
